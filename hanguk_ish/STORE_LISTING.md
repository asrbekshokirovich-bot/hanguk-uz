## English (en)

**Subtitle:** Shifts and jobs in Korea

**Short description:** Day shifts and monthly jobs in Korea: find work nearby and check in with a QR code.

**Full description:**

HanPyol is a job app for young people from abroad living in Korea.

• Day and short shifts: see when, where and how much it pays before you apply.
• Monthly jobs: long-term work paid by the month, not by the shift.
• Map: the jobs nearest to you come first.
• QR check-in: scan the code when you arrive and when you leave, and your hours are recorded.
• Chat with the employer: Korean messages are translated automatically.
• Puyl: open the employer's phone number and exact address with puyl. You get 90 P free when you sign up, and every month you are topped up to 90 P. Invite friends to get more.

**Keywords:** jobs,Korea,part-time,shift,alba,arbeit,student,Seoul,work,monthly job
