## The rules that shape this application (verified)

- **One team → one direction.** You cannot spread Xotira across multiple AI
  Award directions. We chose **Healthcare** (highest social impact, clearest
  KPIs, and it reframes "always-listening" as *care* instead of surveillance).
- **Team:** 3–8 members, each 18+, Uzbek citizens or permanent residents.
- **Prize per direction:** 1st **$100,000** · 2nd **$60,000** · 3rd **$40,000**.
- **Funding is paid in two tranches** tied to performance indicators — hence
  `04-kpi-funding-milestones.md`.
- **Stages:** registration 1 Jul–1 Aug → screening → acceleration program →
  finals & awards before year-end.
- **Second shot:** the same product can *also* enter the **President Tech
  Award** (separate competition, deadline **30 Aug**). Reuse this package.

## Honest risk notes (the jury will ask — have answers ready)

1. **Privacy / always-on recording.** Biggest objection. Answer is baked into
   the product: on-device consent + recording indicator, encryption, data
   stored **in Uzbekistan**, user-controlled deletion. See concept brief §Privacy.
2. **Hardware in 3 days is impossible — and that's fine.** You apply at
   *concept/prototype* stage. Lead with the AI (software) and a reference-hardware
   plan; the award funds you to build it.
3. **"Is the AI real?"** This is an *AI* award — judges probe the model. Be
   specific: Uzbek/Russian/English speech-to-text, speaker diarization, a
   semantic-memory vector store, and LLM retrieval ("what did the doctor say?").
