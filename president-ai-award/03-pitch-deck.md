## Slide 2 — The human problem
A photo of an elderly person + a worried family member.
- Uzbekistan's population is aging.
- Memory decline and dementia steal independence, safety, and dignity.
- Missed medication. Forgotten doctor's orders. Forgotten faces.

> Note: open with one real, human story (a grandparent). Make them feel it.

## Slide 3 — Why today's tools fail
- Paper notes and phone reminders don't *understand* the day.
- The whole burden falls on an exhausted family caregiver.
- Nothing lets the patient simply *ask* what happened.

## Slide 4 — Meet Xotira
Product hero shot (wrist device + phone app).
*A wearable that remembers your day — so you don't have to.*
Remember → Understand → Recall → Reassure.

## Slide 5 — How it works (the AI)
Simple left-to-right diagram:
**Capture (on-device, consented)** → **Speech-to-text (UZ/RU/EN)** →
**Speaker ID** → **Semantic memory (vector store)** → **LLM recall (RAG)** →
**Answers + daily caregiver summary**.

> Note: this is the slide the AI judges care about. Be specific and confident.

## Slide 6 — A day with Xotira
Three real questions, answered:
- *"Did I take my morning pills?"* → "Yes, at 8:10, after breakfast."
- *"What did Dr. Karimova say?"* → "Reduce salt; blood-pressure check in 2 weeks."
- *"Who visited today?"* → "Your neighbor Alisher, around noon."

## Slide 7 — Privacy is the product
- Consent + visible recording indicator, one-tap pause.
- On-device pre-filtering; encryption everywhere.
- **Data stored in Uzbekistan.** User & caregiver control: view, export, delete.

> Note: address the elephant in the room head-on. Turn the objection into trust.
