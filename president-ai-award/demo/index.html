How it works

Not a recorder. An understanding memory.

Everything is designed around one loop: capture the day with consent, turn it into structured memory with AI, and make it answerable in plain language.

01

Capture

On-device, consented listening with a visible indicator and one-tap pause.

02

Understand

Uzbek/Russian/English speech-to-text, plus speaker ID — patient vs. doctor vs. family.

03

Remember

Each moment is embedded into a private semantic memory of events, people & instructions.

04

Recall

Ask anything; an LLM answers from the memory and cites the exact moment.

Privacy is the product

A device that hears everything must be trusted with everything.

Trust isn't a feature bolted on at the end — it's the foundation, and it's what makes an always-available memory acceptable for a vulnerable user.

  • Consent + visible recording indicator, one-tap pause
  • On-device pre-filtering before anything is uploaded
  • Encryption in transit and at rest
  • Data stored in Uzbekistan — sovereign by design
  • User & caregiver control: view, export, delete any memory
  • Clear third-party consent signaling
