# Xotira — KPIs & Two-Tranche Funding Plan

> President AI Award winners are funded in **two equal tranches tied to
> performance indicators**. This is the plan that shows the jury you can turn
> money into measurable impact. Replace `[bracketed]` targets with numbers you
> can actually commit to — under-promise, then beat it.

---

## Funding logic

- **Target prize:** 1st place, **$100,000** (Healthcare direction).
- **Tranche 1 (~$50,000):** paid on award — build the product and launch the pilot.
- **Tranche 2 (~$50,000):** released when the KPIs below are hit.

Keep Tranche-2 KPIs ambitious but *provable within the program window*.
